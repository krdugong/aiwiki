Editorial standards

  • Prices are checked against each vendor's official pricing page, and the check date is shown on the page.
  • We do not state figures we could not confirm from official sources. Where a vendor shows prices only at checkout, we say so and send readers to the official page.
  • Affiliate links carry rel="sponsored", and every page shows an affiliate disclosure, following the Korea Fair Trade Commission endorsement guidelines.
  • Commissions do not change scores or rankings. Tools without an affiliate program are reviewed by the same criteria.
